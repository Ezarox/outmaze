The essentials

How to outmaze your opponent

You are not trying to escape. You are designing the slower maze.

  1. 1
    Shape the maze.

    The runner ignores hazards when choosing a path. It simply takes the shortest open route to the finish.

  2. 2
    Force painful pathing.

    Use 2×2 walls for structure, 1×1 walls for precise choke points, and hazards to disrupt your runner and force a slower escape time.

  3. 3
    Make every second count.

    Effects stack - shape your maze to combine the effects of hazards to slow your runner. You may leave walls or your hazard unused and start whenever you are ready. The longer runner time wins.
